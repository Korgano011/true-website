SMS Consent

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Daly Diligence LLC sends SMS messages to LLC members and other users who have established a relationship with Daly Diligence LLC and have agreed to receive SMS messages.

Message Purpose

Messages may present governance questions, notify recipients that a vote is open, provide voting deadlines, and confirm that a response has been recorded. Recipients may be asked to reply YES or NO.

Consent

By providing a mobile telephone number and agreeing to receive SMS messages, the recipient consents to receive governance voting messages from Daly Diligence LLC. Consent is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out and Help

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. Reply START to subscribe again after opting out.

Privacy

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

Contact

Email dd@dalydiligence.com for assistance.
